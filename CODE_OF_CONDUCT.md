# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone. We act and interact in ways that contribute to an open,
welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Positive behavior includes being respectful of differing viewpoints, giving and
accepting constructive feedback, accepting responsibility for mistakes, and
focusing on what is best for the project and its community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments,
public or private abuse, publishing private information without permission, or
other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the maintainers at opensource@shopify.com.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct. Maintainers
will review reports promptly and fairly while respecting the privacy and
security of the reporter.
